## Which travel technology agencies advertise Expedia Rapid integration services and say they assist with supplier contracting, onboarding or certification?

### Takeaway
The relevant players are not on Fiverr: Expedia itself runs a Certified Technology Partners Program (tiers Elite and Preferred), and three vendors publicly claim that status: ZentrumHub (Elite), Technoheaven (Preferred) and wbe.travel (certified since 2019). Technoheaven and ZentrumHub both describe handling the Expedia onboarding side for the client; a second ring of generic travel-portal agencies advertises Rapid integration without any stated role in approval.

### Inferences
- For this client (adapter already written, application rejected), the certified partners are the only category found whose business model includes the Expedia relationship itself. They are the closest thing to "someone with an inside contact", because Expedia vets and ranks them and they already have partner managers.
- **RISK FLAG, ZentrumHub "pre-integrated connection" / "No Partner Vetting Queue" / onboarding "Not needed":** this is access under the vendor's own Expedia connection, i.e. a form of resold or intermediated access. It may be a legitimate arrangement for an Elite partner, but the client would not hold its own Expedia contract, would depend on the vendor commercially, would route bookings through the vendor's platform instead of its own adapter, and it is not confirmed that Expedia allows a company it has just declined to trade this way. Ask in writing who the contracting party with Expedia is and whether Expedia is told who the end client is.
- **RISK FLAG, Technoheaven "test credentials via Technoheaven":** ambiguous. It may mean Technoheaven sponsors the client's own application (good) or lends its own test keys (weaker). Ask which.
- Second-ring agencies (Amar Infotech, PHPTRAVELS, SRDV, OneClick, Travelopro, FlightsLogic) look like coding shops plus content marketing. Nothing in the snippets shows them contracting with Expedia on the client's behalf.

## Does anyone explicitly offer help getting approved by Expedia, or an introduction to an Expedia account manager?

### Takeaway
No freelancer or agency was found that explicitly sells "we get you approved by Expedia" or "we introduce you to an Expedia account manager". The nearest offers are ZentrumHub (skip Expedia vetting by using its connection) and Technoheaven (credentials and documentation "via Technoheaven"); one unidentified Fiverr gig lists "Rapid API & EPS Launch Technical Setup", which reads as go-live technical help, not approval.

### Cited Findings
- A search for consultants who help with the Rapid partner application or offer account-manager introductions returned no such service; results were vendor marketing pages — [Expedia Rapid API partner page](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/solutions/build-your-travel-experience/rapid-api) (the only official page in that result set; UNVERIFIED, not opened)
- Expedia's "Technical Integration Consultant" is an internal Expedia role that advises existing partners on design, implementation, testing and operation of Rapid integrations: it is post-approval support, not a route in — [Expedia job listing](https://jobs.traveltechessentialist.com/companies/expedia/jobs/90257938-technical-integration-consultant)
- Go-live is controlled by Expedia's own staff: the partner asks its business development manager for a site review, and that manager enables the API key for production — [Expedia Developer Hub: getting started](https://developers.expediagroup.com/docs/products/rapid/setup/getting-started)
- The API key stays in restricted development mode until the site review is passed — [Expedia Developer Hub: Rapid setup](https://developers.expediagroup.com/rapid/setup)
- Expedia publishes separate launch requirements for B2B standalone integrations (travel agents booking for travelers), the model that fits a B2B agent tool — [Expedia Developer Hub: B2B standalone launch requirements](https://developers.expediagroup.com/rapid/setup/launch-requirements/b2b-standalone)
- For add-on APIs Expedia says the account manager or Partner Connect representative requests access approval and amends the agreement, showing that approval runs through Expedia's own representative — [Expedia Developer Hub: Typeahead](https://developers.expediagroup.com/rapid/lodging/typeahead/typeahead)
- ZentrumHub claims "No Partner Vetting Queue" and Expedia onboarding "Not needed" through its platform — [ZentrumHub](https://www.zentrumhub.com/expedia-hotel-api/)
- Technoheaven says test credentials and Rapid documentation are received "via Technoheaven" — [Technoheaven](https://www.technoheaven.com/expedia-xml-api-integration.aspx)
- A Fiverr gig lists "Rapid API & EPS Launch Technical Setup" (exact gig not confirmed) — [candidate gig](https://www.fiverr.com/s/rE2o2Ex)
- A third-party guide says getting credentials requires a partner application with a business case and projected booking volume, and that there is no data-only tier — [StayAPI: Expedia Rapid API, who qualifies](https://stayapi.com/blog/expedia-rapid-api)
- A third-party review describes the path as: apply, be approved, certify the integration, pass technical and commercial review before going live — [Supergood API report card](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/expedia-affiliate-network)

### Inferences
- Because approval is a commercial decision by Expedia's partner team (business case and projected volume), a coder cannot deliver it. Someone who can realistically influence it is either (a) an Expedia certified technology partner that vouches for or carries the client, or (b) a person with a current relationship to an Expedia B2B business development manager. Category (b) was not found advertised anywhere on the marketplaces searched.
- Anyone on Fiverr or Upwork who says "I will give you my Expedia API key" or "use my account" should be treated as a serious risk: the key belongs to another company's contract, Expedia's site review is done per partner, bookings and payments would legally sit with the key owner, and the key can be revoked without notice. **No listing was found that states this in its visible snippet**, but full gig descriptions could not be read, so this must be asked directly before hiring.
- A realistic brief for a hired professional is narrower than "get me approved": review the rejected questionnaire answers, restate the business case and volume forecast, and re-approach Expedia through a named regional B2B contact or through a certified technology partner.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of any independent consultant (ex-Expedia or otherwise) publicly offering Rapid application help. LinkedIn was not searched and may be the better place to find ex-Expedia B2B partnership managers.
- Expedia's contract terms on sharing or sub-licensing API credentials were not seen, so the legal status of "pre-integrated connection" models is not confirmed.
- No information found on Expedia's re-application rules or cooling-off period after a rejection.

## Typical price ranges and what is included

### Takeaway
Marketplace gigs that name Expedia start at $5 to $200 and reach about $995 for a full single-supplier hotel integration; an Upwork catalog listing shows a $5,000 / 60-day placeholder. All of these cover code only, and Expedia itself publishes no fee for Rapid access. Certified-partner platforms charge an unpublished flat SaaS fee.

### Cited Findings

Both are vendor claims.

### Inferences
- What is included at marketplace prices: search, details, booking and confirmation code, sometimes a website or plugin, source code hand-over. What is not included anywhere visible: Expedia contracting, the questionnaire, the business case, the site review or launch-requirement compliance work.
- A $200 price for five or more supplier integrations in 21 days is not credible for a compliant Rapid B2B integration; such gigs most likely deliver search-and-display code against keys the buyer supplies.
- Since the client's adapter exists, paying marketplace rates for more code is poor value. Money is better spent on (a) a paid review of the rejected application by someone with Expedia B2B experience, or (b) a certified technology partner's platform fee, after the risk questions above are answered.
