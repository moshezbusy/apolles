# Reaching a human at Expedia Group B2B (Rapid API): contacts, consultants and official channels

Research date: 26 November 2026. Method note for the report writer: this session could not open pages directly (partner.expediagroup.com and developers.expediagroup.com were outside the session's allowed network, so WebFetch failed). Everything below comes from web-search result summaries and snippets, not from reading the full pages. Treat every name and title as "found in a search snippet, to be confirmed on the live page or LinkedIn profile before use". No client details were used in any query.

## 1. Who at Expedia Group B2B is publicly identified as handling Rapid API new business / partner acquisition for EMEA, Middle East, Israel or small and mid-size partners? What titles should one search for on LinkedIn?

No publicly named person was found with a title like "Business Development, Rapid API, EMEA / Middle East / Israel". ## 2. Does Expedia have an office or representative for Israel? Who are known Expedia market managers in Israel who could make an internal introduction?

### Takeaway
No Expedia office in Israel and no named Israel market manager could be confirmed. The only regional supply-side names found are the Dubai-based Area Manager Middle East and the Athens-based Director for Greece, Cyprus and East Adriatic, and neither is confirmed to cover Israel.

### Cited Findings
- "Expedia does not have an office in Israel or the OPT, but it employs staff who manage its business there", including account management staff who seek new properties. This is from 2019 and may be outdated. — [Amnesty International, 2019](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/campaigns/2019/01/chapter-4-online-tourism-companies-doing-business-in-the-settlements/)
- Hajer Ben El Hedi — Area Manager Middle East, Expedia Group, based in Dubai, in the role since August 2023 (previously Senior Market Manager, regional chains, Middle East). Lodging supply side. Israel is not named in the snippet. — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hajer-ben-el-hedi-b2920a2b/)
- Director, Market Management for Greece, Cyprus & East Adriatic: one conference page says Irene Roberts has held this since June 2024; other pages list Vassiliki (Vasiliki) Mavrokefalou with the same title, with a remit of Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria and East Adriatic. The sources conflict on who holds it now; Roberts appears to be the more recent. Israel is not in the stated remit. — [IMH Business, Roberts](https://realestate.imhbusiness.com/?p=1697); [IMH Business, Mavrokefalou](https://www.imhbusiness.com/?p=7530); [Portfolio.hu speaker page](https://portfolio.hu/en/events/speaker/vassiliki-mavrokefalou/7863)
- Expedia's travel-agent programme (TAAP) has per-country "Account Management Support" contacts; the support page seen in search lists only the United States, Ireland and Canada, and no Israel contact appeared. — [Expedia TAAP Academy support](https://taapacademy.expediagroup.com/support); [TAAP FAQ](https://taapacademy.expediagroup.com/faq)
- Historic only: about twenty years ago Expedia planned its Israel section through local operator IGT. Not usable as a current contact. — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/Travel/Travel-News/Expedia-to-launch-Israeli-travel-site)
- For contrast, Booking.com does have a Tel Aviv office (opened 2021). — [Booking.com careers](https://careers.booking.com/tel-aviv)

### Inferences
- The most realistic local route is indirect: Israeli hotel chains and hotel revenue managers each have an assigned Expedia market manager. Asking a friendly Israeli hotel for the name of its Expedia market manager is likely to identify the person faster than any public search.
- If the agency already books through Expedia TAAP, its TAAP account-management contact is an existing human relationship inside the same B2B division as Rapid.

### Gaps
- No Expedia employee with "Israel" in a publicly indexed title was found, in English or Hebrew. Israeli trade press (Passport News, IAS) returned nothing naming an Expedia representative.
- Which Expedia market-management team covers Israeli hotels (Middle East from Dubai, or a Mediterranean/Europe team) is not publicly stated.
- Whether TAAP has an Israel account manager could not be established.

## 3. Which consultants or advisory firms (ex-Expedia, ex-Hotelbeds, travel distribution advisers) advertise helping startups and agencies obtain API supply deals?

### Takeaway
No independent consultant or boutique advisory firm was found that publicly advertises getting companies approved for Expedia Rapid or brokering supplier API contracts. The parties that do publicly offer this kind of help are Expedia's own Certified Technology Partners, which are technology vendors with a commercial interest, not neutral advisers.

### Cited Findings
- Expedia runs a Certified Technology Partners Program for businesses that lack the resources to connect to Rapid API or prefer to outsource the integration; partners are tiered (Elite, Preferred) on business metrics and integration quality. Expedia states that choosing a technology partner is the partner's own commercial decision and that it is not responsible for those vendors. — [Expedia, Certified Technology Partners Program](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/solutions/build-your-travel-experience/rapid-api/certified-technology-partners-program)
- The partner directory lists 14 partners; Elite tier named in search results: Juniper Travel Technology, ZentrumHub, Atriis, DCS Plus, Qtech, Travelgate, GIATA, Vervotech. Preferred tier includes Technoheaven, Cangooroo, Switchfly. The directory's date is not shown. — [Expedia Group B2B technology partners directory](https://technologypartners.expediagroup.com/)
- Conflict: a second search confirmed Atriis as a certified Rapid technology partner but found no separate source confirming Elite status. — [Expedia, Certified Technology Partners Program](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/solutions/build-your-travel-experience/rapid-api/certified-technology-partners-program)
- Travelgate announced Elite Partner status with Expedia Group; Vervotech announced Elite Technology Partner status in October 2026. — [Travelgate blog](https://blog.travelgate.com/travelgate-is-now-elite-partner-of-expedia-group); [Breaking Travel News](https://www.breakingtravelnews.com/news/article/vervotech-recognized-as-an-elite-technology-partner-by-expedia-group/)
- ZentrumHub (vendor claim) says it offers a "bring your own contract" model where the client signs directly with suppliers and connects through its platform, and says it can facilitate introductions to suppliers. — [ZentrumHub hotel API page](https://www.zentrumhub.com/hotel-api/)
- Expedia says a partner does not have to use a technology partner and can connect to the API directly. — [Expedia, Certified Technology Partners Program](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/solutions/build-your-travel-experience/rapid-api/certified-technology-partners-program)
- Weak individual lead: Sofia Vasiliadou (Greece) — LinkedIn shows Expedia Group (Solutions & Capabilities team) and a post saying she runs her own advisory and consulting practice taking "fractional, interim, and strategic growth projects" alongside Channel-Sense.AI. The snippets are undated, do not confirm whether she still works at Expedia, and do not say she helps with API partnerships. — [LinkedIn](https://gr.linkedin.com/in/sofia-vasiliadou-99572433)

### Inferences
- A technology partner with Elite status has its own Expedia business development contact and a track record of bringing clients onto Rapid, so it is the closest thing to a documented "insider introduction". The trade-off is that the vendor will want the client to use its platform.
- Atriis is the technology partner on the list most likely to be relevant to an Israeli company, but its Israeli base was not confirmed by the sources retrieved in this session and should be checked.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable source for any ex-Expedia Partner Solutions, ex-Hotelbeds or ex-WebBeds person publicly selling supplier-introduction or API-partnership advisory. Several searches returned only vendor marketing pages.
- Well-known travel-technology advisers exist (for example T2Impact, Travel Tech Consulting, Travel Tech Essentialist), but these names come from general industry knowledge, were not verified in this session, and nothing found indicates they broker Expedia introductions. They should not be presented as confirmed options.
- LinkedIn is the natural place such freelancers advertise ("ex-Expedia", "fractional partnerships"), and it could not be searched directly.

## 4. What are the official escalation or contact routes besides the application form, and where does Expedia B2B staff meet prospective partners?

### Takeaway
No public email address or phone number for Rapid API sales or for appealing a rejection was found; the documented routes are web forms and, once approved, an assigned business development manager. The most concrete in-person options are Expedia's own Explore partner conference and the large trade shows, though no evidence was found that Expedia B2B staffs a stand at any of them.

### Cited Findings

## 5. Any public accounts of companies that were rejected and then approved after reaching a person, and how they did it?

### Takeaway
None found. There are no Reddit, Hacker News, Quora or LinkedIn accounts in the search results of a company being rejected for Rapid and later approved. Only second-hand vendor descriptions of the selection criteria exist.

### Cited Findings
- Expedia does not publish its enrolment terms; a third-party guide says it considers revenue and growth potential, the applicant's existing solution, website traffic and average number of bookings. — [AltexSoft](https://www.altexsoft.com/blog/expedia-taap-rapid-api-partner-solutions/)
- Getting credentials requires a partner application with a business case and projected booking volume; data-only or monitoring use cases generally do not qualify because Rapid earns from bookings. — [StayAPI blog](https://stayapi.com/blog/expedia-rapid-api)
- Vendor claim: Expedia vets applicants on booking volume, market and coverage, and "smaller platforms are frequently turned away"; individual developers and non-travel businesses do not qualify. ZentrumHub sells access to Rapid as a technology partner, so this is self-interested. — [ZentrumHub](https://www.zentrumhub.com/expedia-hotel-api/)
- Vendor claim: even when accepted, enrolment, contract negotiation and connection can take several months. — [ZentrumHub blog](https://www.zentrumhub.com/blog/expedia-rapid-hotel-api-integration/)
- Access is partner-only (application, approval, certification) with no self-serve signup. — [Supergood API report card](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/expedia-affiliate-network)
- Expedia's published rejection and re-application guidance (incomplete information, website not live; applicants may reapply) belongs to the Travel Creator Program, a different programme, and may not apply to Rapid. — [Expedia Creator help centre](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/15361858567191-Can-I-resubmit-an-application-after-being-rejected)

### Inferences
- An automated rejection straight after the questionnaire most likely reflects the screening inputs (declared booking volume, website traffic, live product), not a human judgement. A re-approach is more likely to work if it presents a stronger business case with booking projections and a live product, or arrives through a certified technology partner.
- There is no evidence of a formal appeal process for Rapid.

### Gaps
- No first-hand success story to model the approach on. Absence in search results does not prove none exist; closed LinkedIn posts and private communities are not indexed.
- Expedia's actual minimum thresholds for Rapid (volume, traffic) are not public.
- Whether a rejected applicant is barred from reapplying for a period is unknown.
