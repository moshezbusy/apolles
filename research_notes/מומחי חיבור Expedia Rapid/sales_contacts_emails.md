Research date: 26 November 2026. No client details were used in any query.

Method note for the report writer. Direct page loading (WebFetch) failed for every host tried (ias.co.il, ittn.ie, taapacademy.expediagroup.com, expedia.com, partner.expediagroup.com). Pages marked **[read]** below were read in full through a page-parsing tool. Items marked **[snippet]** come only from a Google or search-tool result snippet and the page itself was not opened. This file does not repeat the earlier pass (Paredes, Cheng, Bolda, Laborie, the supply-side market managers, event dates).

## 1. Which email addresses and phone lines are publicly listed for Rapid / Expedia Group B2B / EPS / TAAP?

### Takeaway
There is no published sales or support mailbox for Rapid API or Expedia Partner Solutions; those routes are web forms and a sign-in portal only. The only published B2B mailboxes are the per-country Expedia TAAP "account management support" addresses (34 points of sale plus one catch-all for the rest of Europe), and none of them is for Israel.

## 5. Is there a documented escalation, appeal or re-review route for a declined Rapid application?

### Takeaway
No. Expedia publishes no appeal, re-review or reconsideration procedure for Rapid. What is documented is that applications are "reviewed case by case" and that unsuitable applicants may be handed to a downstream API partner.

### Cited Findings
- **[read]** The application form states: "All applications will be reviewed case by case." — [Get started with Rapid API](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/join-us/rapid-api)
- **[read]** The same form carries this consent line: "If my business is not suitable to connect directly with Expedia Group B2B, I agree that Expedia Group B2B may pass my details to one of its API downstream partners, who may then reach out to me by email about accessing Expedia Group supply via their own connection to the Expedia Group B2B API." — [Get started with Rapid API](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/join-us/rapid-api)
- **[read]** The EPS support portal is addressed to existing B2B partners; it allows a new user to "sign up for an account using the signup link" and has a request form. Nothing on it mentions applications or appeals. — [EPS support article](https://support.expediapartnersolutions.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014272939-Are-you-a-B2B-Partner-Sign-into-our-Support-Portal-to-get-in-touch); [EPS support portal](https://support.expediapartnersolutions.com/)
- **[snippet]** Expedia does publish rejection, resubmission and appeal articles, with a mailbox, for its Travel Creator Program only. That is a different programme. — [Expedia Creator help centre, Account Suspension Appeals](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/sections/33196161539479-Account-Suspension-Appeals)
- **[read]** Expedia's TAAP vice-president describes the API as the product for "businesses with existing booking interfaces" and TAAP as the product for travel agencies, within one division. — [TTN Middle East, 1 June 2025](https://ttnonline.com/Article/385879/-Expedia-debuts-travel-agent-platform-in-UAE-)

### Inferences
- The downstream-partner clause is Expedia's official answer to "not suitable for a direct connection": access through an existing Rapid partner's connection. If that box was ticked, an approach from such a partner may arrive by email; if not, re-submitting the form with it ticked is the documented fallback.
- Since review is "case by case" and done by people in business development, a direct message to a named business development person is the practical substitute for an appeal.
- There is nothing published that forbids a second application.

### Gaps
- No published waiting period, threshold or re-application rule for Rapid.
- Unknown whether a non-partner can open a ticket in the EPS support portal and get a reply.
- Which "API downstream partners" receive referred applicants is not published.
