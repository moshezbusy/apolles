Automatic emails

Every email the system sends by itself, who gets it, when, and what it says. Emails to agents come from Apolles. Emails to clients carry only the agency brand, come from a neutral domain and never mention Apolles.

Template A · to agents (Apolles brand)

From: Apolles <no-reply@apolles.com>

Apolles

Your client sent their choices

Daniel Levy selected 2 options in "Mexico Honeymoon". Prices can change, so book soon.

Open quote
Apolles · You get this email because you have an Apolles agency account.
Template B · to clients (agency brand only)

From: My Agency <my-agency@(client domain)>
Reply-to: moshe@myagency.com

MA
My AgencyMoshe Zafrani · Travel advisor

Your travel proposal: Mexico Honeymoon

Hi Daniel, here are the hotel options I prepared for you. Choose what you like and send me your choice.

View your proposal
Sent by My Agency · Reply to this email to reach Moshe Zafrani.

Logo, name and color come from Agency settings. The language comes from the quote. Sent from the neutral client domain, never from apolles.com. Replies go straight to the agent.

Rules for all emails

  • Not sent: an email when the client opens a quote. The agent sees views inside Apolles.
  • Automatic cancellation happens 24 hours before the free cancellation ends (hotel time), only for bookings without a voucher, and only when the cancellation charge is USD 0.00.
  • Agent emails are in English. Client emails follow the quote language (English or Hebrew).
  • Client emails never show Apolles, the supplier, or net prices.
  • Client domain: emails to clients are sent from a neutral domain without the word "apolles" (placeholder: (client domain)), with the agency name as the sender and the agent's email as reply-to. The technical return address (bounces) is on the same domain. Client quote links and voucher files use the same domain.
  • Times of events (sent, booked, paid) in the agent's time zone. Cancellation deadlines and automatic cancellation times in hotel time with the time zone, as on the booking pages. 24-hour format.
  • Every email has a plain-text version and works on mobile.
